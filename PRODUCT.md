# DC Explorer

## Platform
Web. Next.js application, hosted on Vercel.

## Product
Explore Washington, DC as an open 3D world. The user has explicitly approved a first playable route from Lincoln Memorial along the Reflecting Pool to Washington Monument, with Three.js rendering and Rapier character movement.

## Confirmed constraints
Preserve the incumbent visual language: warm cream surfaces, terracotta edges, tactile controls, discovery and tour features. Prioritize exploration, responsiveness, architecture, and grounded movement. Existing Mapbox city exploration remains accessible.

## First route
An original architectural interpretation of the National Mall, not a survey-grade digital twin. Asset detail and geographical coverage must be described honestly. The world must run without new paid-service credentials. Audience beyond people interested in exploring DC is unspecified.

## Approved expansion

User approved neighborhood streaming, original-theme alignment, and continuous Mall-to-Capitol exploration. They supplied a detailed stylized streetscape reference and explicitly required fidelity to DC. Use sourced DC geography, distinguish illustrative facades/heights from real geometry, and do not substitute another city's architecture or interface language. Full-city coverage remains the longer-term objective.

## Full District scope correction

The user explicitly rejected corridor-only delivery. The required geographic scope is the whole District, including continuously visible distant neighborhoods, waterways, parks, and travel beyond the Mall. Preserve the incumbent design and distinguish complete geographic coverage from exact architectural/elevation fidelity.

## Experience confirmed September 2026

The intended product is a stylized, playable, open-world DC centered on exploration and discovery. Preserve the original cream-and-terracotta gamified HUD. Real geographic data supports recognizable neighborhoods, enjoyable movement, and detail; data inspection is not the product. `/survey` remains a development comparison and is not promoted from the game HUD. Neighborhood discoveries complement the Mall route; whole-District walking, flight, and fast travel remain core.
